About Catering Spares

A proudly Australian business supplying genuine and aftermarket spare parts for professional catering equipment — backed by more than 20 years of expertise.

WHO WE ARE

Catering to your requirements, handling your needs with care

Our store is more than just another average online retailer. We sell not only top-quality products, but give our customers a positive online shopping experience.

Catering Spares was established in 2008 with the goal of bringing the whole range of commercial catering equipment spare parts direct to service companies.

We've built strong relationships with equipment manufacturers, so we can source a comprehensive range of genuine and replacement spare parts. By continually researching the equipment market, we keep our stock and parts up to date.

As one of the largest catering equipment suppliers in Australia, we bring our expertise to every order — providing high-quality support so you can operate your business smoothly and enjoy a genuine partnership with us.
